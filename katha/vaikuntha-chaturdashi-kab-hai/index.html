In English

Vaikuntha Chaturdashi 2026 is on Monday, 23 November. The puja is in Nishita kaal, 11:41 PM to 12:35 AM in New Delhi (Drik Panchang). It marks Hari-Har milan: Vishnu offered a thousand lotuses to Shiva, offered his own eye when one was missing, and received the Sudarshan chakra. Only on this day is tulsi offered to Shiva and bel leaves to Vishnu.
