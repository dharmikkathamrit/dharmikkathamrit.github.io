In English

Kaal Bhairav Jayanti 2026 is on Tuesday, 1 December. Ashtami runs from 12:11 AM to 11:13 PM on 1 December (Drik Panchang, New Delhi). Bhairav puja is done at night, so the Jayanti is kept on 1 December. Light a mustard-oil diya in the evening, recite the Kalabhairav Ashtakam, keep the night vigil and feed a black dog a roti with mustard oil.
