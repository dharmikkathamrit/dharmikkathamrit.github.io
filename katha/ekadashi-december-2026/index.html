In English

Utpanna Ekadashi is on Friday 4 December 2026 (parana Saturday 5 December, 6:59 to 9:04 AM) and Mokshada Ekadashi with Gita Jayanti on Sunday 20 December 2026 (parana Monday 21 December, 7:10 to 9:13 AM), New Delhi times from Drik Panchang.
